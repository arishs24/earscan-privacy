EarScan Privacy Policy

Effective November 4, 2026

EarScan is an iPhone app that photographs an ear and sends that scan to a laptop you choose, so a 3D model can be built for a hearing-aid device. This policy describes what leaves the phone.

The app is published by Arish Shahab. Contact: arish.shahab1@gmail.com.

Support

For help with EarScan, email arish.shahab1@gmail.com.

Data the app collects

Nothing is sent until you use Send to laptop. The upload goes only to the laptop address you enter. It is kept there so the 3D model can be built. There are no advertising or analytics services in the app.

  • Photos. The ear photographs, and the depth maps that go with them, are uploaded as part of the scan.
  • Health information. The scan is used to make a hearing-aid device. It is not sent to a health network, insurer, or research study.
  • Device ID. The phone’s vendor identifier is included in the session file. It tells which physical phone a depth calibration belongs to.

The app does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, payment information, or browsing history. It does not record your voice. Spoken prompts are played by the phone.

How the data is used

The scan is used only to run the app: to reconstruct the ear and keep a phone’s depth calibration with that phone. It is not used for advertising, marketing, analytics, or personalization, and it is not sold.

The data is not linked to an account. EarScan has no user accounts.

Where the data is stored

Scans stay in the EarScan folder on the iPhone until you delete them in the app or remove the app. A scan you send is also stored on the laptop you selected. Whoever operates that laptop is responsible for keeping or deleting that copy.

Camera and local network

The camera is used to photograph the ear. Local network access is used to find the laptop on your Wi-Fi and to send the scan. The app does not browse the web.

Children

EarScan is not directed at children, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from children through an account.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
