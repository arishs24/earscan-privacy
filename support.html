EarScan Support

Help for the EarScan iPhone app

EarScan photographs an ear and can send that scan to a laptop you choose. The laptop builds a 3D model.

Contact

Email arish@amanolabs.com. Include your iPhone model and a short description of what happened.

Sending a scan

  • Allow the camera when iOS asks.
  • Put the iPhone and the laptop on the same Wi-Fi network.
  • In Settings, enter the laptop address and pairing code, then use Find laptop or Test.
  • After the scan, choose Send to laptop.

Photos on the iPhone

Scans stay in EarScan until you delete them in the app. Deleting the app removes the copies stored on that iPhone. A scan already sent to a laptop stays on that laptop until it is deleted there.
